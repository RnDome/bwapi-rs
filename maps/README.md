# Maps

- `scbw`: the maps of StarCraft and Brood War by Blizzard Entertainment, as the
  BWAPI distribution carries them (melee, ladder, allied and web maps).
- `scai`: the tournament maps bots play, from the tests of
  [Stardust](https://github.com/bmnielsen/Stardust) and of
  [BWEM-community](https://github.com/N00byEdge/BWEM-community). Its Showdown
  is not the ladder Showdown of `scbw`.
- `bwem`: the synthetic test maps of BWEM-community.
